Neighborhood GuideMount Pleasant, SC

old mount pleasant

Old Mount Pleasant blends coastal Lowcountry charm with tree-lined streets, historic architecture, and a walkable village atmosphere along the East Cooper waterfront.

A Lowcountry Village with Deep Roots

Old Mount Pleasant is the historic core of Mount Pleasant, South Carolina, situated along the banks of Shem Creek and the Charleston Harbor just across the Arthur Ravenel Jr. Bridge from downtown Charleston. One of the oldest continuously settled communities in the region, its streets carry a sense of quiet history that newer developments simply cannot replicate. Moss-draped oaks arch over sidewalks, and the pace of life here is unhurried in the way that only genuinely established places tend to be.

Architecture and Housing Character

The neighborhood's built environment is a study in Lowcountry vernacular architecture. Cottages and bungalows dating from the early-to-mid twentieth century sit alongside larger Charleston-style homes with deep porches designed to catch the sea breeze. Many properties have been carefully maintained or thoughtfully restored, preserving original details like heart pine floors, transomed doorways, and single-pile floor plans. The streetscape is intimate and human-scaled, a contrast to the larger planned subdivisions that surround the area.

Shem Creek and the Waterfront

Shem Creek is the neighborhood's defining geographic feature — a tidal estuary that has served as a working waterfront for generations. Today it balances its heritage as a shrimping hub with a vibrant lineup of waterfront restaurants, kayak launches, and a paved boardwalk that winds along the marsh's edge. Watching shrimp boats navigate the creek at dusk alongside kayakers and wading herons captures something essential about Old Mount Pleasant's character: working history and natural beauty occupying the same water.

Walkability and Local Life

Old Mount Pleasant's village center along Coleman Boulevard and Pitt Street offers a walkable cluster of locally owned shops, cafés, and restaurants that give the area a distinct, non-franchised personality. The Pitt Street Bridge, a former rail and auto crossing now converted to a pedestrian promenade, extends into the marsh and is a beloved spot for sunrise walks and fishing. Moultrie Park provides open green space and tennis courts within the neighborhood's footprint, anchoring everyday outdoor life close to home.

Location and Connectivity

Positioned just minutes from downtown Charleston via the Ravenel Bridge, Old Mount Pleasant offers genuine proximity to one of the South's most celebrated historic cities while maintaining its own distinct identity. Sullivan's Island and Isle of Palms beaches are accessible within a short drive, and the broader East Cooper area — with its marinas, nature preserves, and dining corridors — fans out in every direction. It is a neighborhood that feels rooted in place even as everything around it continues to grow.
