About Nathan Overbey

15 Years as an Old Village Specialist

Nathan Overbey is a Mount Pleasant, SC real estate agent and the founder of Overbey Real Estate Group. Having lived in the Old Village neighborhood of Mount Pleasant for more than 13 years, Nathan brings a depth of local knowledge that goes well beyond what any market report can offer — from the quiet streets that feel just right to the small pockets that make this community so distinctive.

For Nathan, real estate is personal. As a family man himself, he understands what it truly means to find a home where life gets lived — where kids grow up, memories are made, and roots go deep. That genuine connection to home and community is what drives his work every single day.

Whether you're buying your first home in Mount Pleasant or selling a property you've loved for years, Nathan's approach is rooted in honesty, care, and an insider's understanding of the area. He takes the time to really listen, matching clients not just with a house, but with the right fit for their lives.

If you're looking for a Mount Pleasant real estate agent who knows the Old Village and the surrounding Lowcountry communities the way a neighbor does, Nathan Overbey and the Overbey Real Estate Group are ready to help you find your way home.
